# User Data Deletion Instructions – Pi MS

**Pi MS (Passive Infrastructure Management System)**  
**Dipa Poros Advisory & Digital Solutions ("Dipa Poros", "we", "us", "our")**

If you use Pi MS and want us to delete personal data associated with you, you can submit a deletion request using the instructions below.

## 1. How to request deletion

Send an email to:

**Email:** support@dipaporos.com
**Subject:** Data Request – Delete My Data

Please include:

- your full name
- the mobile number or email address associated with Pi MS
- your organisation or employer, if applicable
- a clear statement that you want your personal data deleted
- any additional information that helps us identify the relevant Pi MS account or records

For security, we may need to verify your identity before acting on the request.

## 2. What data may be deleted

Depending on how you use Pi MS and the records associated with you, a deletion request may cover personal data such as:

- account and identity information
- contact information
- WhatsApp, SMS or email communication records associated with your use of Pi MS
- alarm acknowledgements, escalation history, comments and other operational activity associated with you
- support requests, complaints and feedback
- technical or session information associated with your account

Pi MS primarily processes machine and infrastructure-site data. Equipment readings, site information and other non-personal operational data may not be considered personal data and may not be deleted as part of an individual data-deletion request.

## 3. Enterprise or employer-managed accounts

If Pi MS is provided to you through your employer, telecom operator, tower company, facility owner or another enterprise customer, Dipa Poros may process your personal data on that customer's behalf.

In that situation, we may forward your deletion request to the relevant customer and assist them in responding to it. You may also contact your employer or the relevant enterprise customer directly.

## 4. Data we may need to retain

We will delete or anonymise personal data that is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, security and record-retention requirements.

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where it is required for purposes such as:

- legal or regulatory compliance
- security and access logging
- contractual or audit records
- resolving disputes or complaints
- maintaining required operational records for an enterprise customer

Where information must be retained, access will remain limited to the purposes for which retention is required.

## 5. WhatsApp messages and opting out

If you only want to stop receiving non-essential Pi MS WhatsApp messages, you may reply **STOP** to a Pi MS WhatsApp message.

Please note that opting out of messages is different from requesting deletion of your personal data. To request deletion, follow the email process in Section 1 above.

## 6. After we receive your request

After receiving a valid deletion request, we will:

1. review the request and identify the relevant personal data;
2. verify your identity where necessary;
3. determine whether any information must be retained under applicable law, contract or customer instructions;
4. delete or anonymise eligible personal data; and
5. respond to you when the request has been addressed.
